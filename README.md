# Noter

Lists of numbered steps you tick off as you go. Each list has its own colour and progress bar, steps can have smaller steps under them, you drag the dots to reorder, and you swipe left to delete.

Everything is in `index.html`. Your lists are saved on the device you use. To sync them between devices, open the side menu, tap **Make a key** under Cloud saving (a GitHub token with only the `gist` permission), paste it in and tap **Connect**. Noter then saves your lists to a private gist called "Noter data". **Back up** and **Restore** save and load a copy as a file.

## Use it on an iPhone

1. Open https://slowone11.github.io/noter2/ in Safari.
2. Tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
